Is Markhor Trips a licensed travel agency?

Yes. Markhor Trips is operated by Delight Express Tourism LLC, an IATA accredited travel agency based in Sharjah, United Arab Emirates.

Can I pay for my trip in instalments?

Yes. Eligible bookings can be split into interest free instalments with Tabby or Tamara. We also accept Visa and Mastercard on our secure checkout.

How long does the Saudi multiple entry visa take?

The Saudi one year multiple entry visa, with stays of up to 90 days, usually takes 10 to 15 working days once all documents are submitted.

Where does the Umrah bus depart from?

Our 10 day Umrah by bus package picks up from Sharjah, Dubai, Ajman and Abu Dhabi, with 6 nights in Makkah and 2 nights in Madinah.

Do you offer support during my trip?

Yes. Our team is available 24 hours a day on +971 6 704 4002, on WhatsApp and by email at support@markhortrips.com.
